Angry Birds meets bubble shooter in a super-addictive puzzle game.

Let the bubbles fly in a charming bubble shooter downloaded by more than 45 MILLION players! Match and pop colorful bubbles in over 2000 captivating levels, and participate in special events to earn awesome rewards!

Join Stella, Red, Chuck, Bomb, and the rest of the Angry Birds flock in a bubble shooting adventure bursting with a nearly endless supply of challenges! Grab your trusty slingshot and shoot those bubbles to drop the pigs and rescue the impossibly adorable Hatchlings. With special guests and events popping up all the time, there’s always a fun new challenge to take on.

Angry Birds POP! is available for download on the App Store, Google Play Store, and Amazon.com.

Responsibilities

  • Point of contact for LiveOps, level creation for Angry Birds Pop, and outsourced QA team, taking full ownership of 10,000+ levels across 15+ events.
  • Managed regular rebalancing processes to maintain player retention and engagement.
  • Collaborated with production, programming, art, and product teams to define event criteria including leaderboards, sidemaps, win-streaks, sales, and season passes.
  • Partnered with Product Management to run game longevity experiments, including interstitial ad testing in Tier 1 countries, level sawtooth optimization, and FTUE improvements to reduce early churn.
  • Represented team as council member, delivering feedback on company operations directly to CEO and HR leadership.
  • Mentored second-year students at Abertay University, sharing industry insights and career guidance.
