Have a blast in the kitchen with Gordon Ramsay!

Gordon Ramsay's Chef Blast is a cube-matching puzzle game where you tap groups of matching blocks to clear them and work through hundreds of cooking-themed levels, with new ones added every couple of weeks. Levels reward smart play — finding efficient solutions earns higher scores and three-star ratings — and completing them pays out in-game currency. That cash goes toward renovating a virtual kitchen, with over 200 upgrades spread across different themes that you can complete individually or mix together however you like. Boosters help you get through tougher stages, and the game works offline for play anywhere.

Beyond the core levels, the game runs rotating weekly events. Baking Bingo has you baking cupcakes to match customer orders for prizes, Hot Stove League pits you against other players to clear levels in the fewest attempts for leaderboard placement, and Market Day involves collecting stickers from specially marked blocks to trade for rewards. There's also a collectible recipe book featuring Ramsay's personal favorites plus dishes exclusive to the game, including a holiday recipe. Social features let you join a team, exchange lives with friends and other players, and compete on individual and team leaderboards.

Gordon Ramsay: Chef Blast is available for download on the App Store, Google Play Store, and Amazon.com.
